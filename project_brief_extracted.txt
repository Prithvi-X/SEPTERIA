4. WHAT SEPTERIA IS

SEPTERIA is a welfare-first, context-aware predictive platform for MHA-administered CAPF personnel. Its core purpose is to identify concerning stress/recovery patterns early by combining organizational context, voluntary wellness information and optional physiological signals, then recommend a proportionate human welfare response.

Core principle:

“Stress is not a single sensor reading. Interpret physiology in the context of the person, the duty, the environment and the trajectory.”

8. AUTHORITY-CONTROLLED OPERATIONAL CONTEXT

Personnel should not be responsible for continuously maintaining authoritative operational facts that are already managed by the organization. The Authority side is the source of operational context.

Permanent context: posting, unit, role, base/location category.

Operational context: duty, shift, deployment, zone, environmental category, workload context.

Temporary context: operation, temporary posting, temporary shift/location, special assignment.

Events: leave started, leave ended, return to duty, incident exposure, deployment ended.

Bulk changes: an authorized officer can update an entire unit or selected personnel at once.

Critical integrity rule: authority may change the input context, but must not directly edit the AI-generated risk score or recommendation.

9. TIME-BOUND ASSIGNMENTS

A temporary operational change must support start time, end time or duration, countdown, extension and automatic reversion to the previous context.

Historical post-leave findings should be framed as evidence motivating additional welfare attention, not as deterministic suicide prediction rules.

12. CORE DATA MODEL

13. DATA QUALITY + EVIDENCE STATUS

The system must distinguish what was measured from what was computed or inferred. Use explicit evidence-status labels:

OBSERVED: directly received from an authorized data source.

DERIVED: calculated from observed data, such as rolling HRV trend or baseline deviation.

INFERRED: estimated or reconstructed from a model/graph/context.

UNCERTAIN: data quality or confidence is insufficient for a strong action.

No inferred value should silently appear identical to an observed measurement.

14. PERSONAL BASELINE

The baseline is a central part of the core intelligence. Do not use a one-size-fits-all HRV or HR threshold.

Prototype approach: rolling statistics and robust deviation features first. If a later model demonstrates clear benefit, more advanced baselines may be evaluated. The detailed solution design recommends starting with a strong interpretable baseline and only adding complexity when validation demonstrates an incremental benefit. fileciteturn18file15

15. MULTIMODAL STRESS / RECOVERY PREDICTION

Core model choice for the prototype: XGBoost, with a clear baseline comparator such as logistic regression. Use subject-wise validation rather than random row-level splits when participant identity is available.

The target for the prototype should be a conservative stress/recovery risk level and/or recovery deterioration indicator. It is not a psychiatric diagnosis and is not a suicide-prediction system.

16. MODEL OUTPUT

Every prediction screen should present:

Risk level: LOW / MODERATE / HIGH.

Confidence: numerical or categorical confidence.

Trajectory: stable / improving / deteriorating.

Evidence status: observed/derived/inferred where relevant.

Top contributing factors: actual model explanation, preferably SHAP or equivalent.

Recommended next action: proportionate welfare response.

17. WELFARE ACTION MODEL

The system recommends and explains; authorized welfare/medical personnel make human intervention decisions.

18. CONTEXTUAL PERSONNEL GRAPH

The graph is a contextual intelligence layer, not a system for sharing raw peer medical data. Relationships can reflect shared duty, shift, zone, environment, workload and similar recovery trajectories.

Prototype uses:

Recover: support cautious interpretation of missing/noisy observations using personal history plus contextually similar evidence.

Detect: identify shared deterioration across personnel exposed to similar operating conditions.

Adapt: provide a contextual prior for cold-start users with limited personal history.

Privacy rule: Share context and aggregates, never raw peer health data. The graph and cooperative imputation are proposed mechanisms and require empirical validation before operational use. fileciteturn18file2

19. VOICE MODULE

Voice should be voluntary and context-triggered or scheduled, never a covert background recording mechanism.

20–30 second natural speech sample.

Local/controlled feature extraction where possible.

Acoustic features: F0/pitch, variability, speech rate, pauses, energy and MFCCs.

Compare against the person’s own voice baseline.

Voice is one evidence stream, not a standalone stress detector.

If microphone quality or confidence is poor, reduce confidence rather than forcing a label.

24. CORE DEMO SCENARIO

The entire prototype should be designed to make this single scenario work reliably:

Authority selects a BSF unit and creates a temporary Zone 2 assignment: border/remote/extreme environment, night duty, high heat, 7 days.

Authority applies the assignment in bulk. The database updates the operational context and the countdown begins.

A selected personnel member is marked as returned from leave. A 14-day post-leave transition window activates.

Synthetic multi-day data shows reduced sleep, declining HRV, rising resting HR and higher workload.

The system runs quality checks, identifies the operational zone, computes personal baseline deviation and evaluates the trajectory.

The trained prediction model returns an elevated stress/recovery risk with confidence and contributing factors.

The system recommends a confidential welfare check-in rather than making a clinical or disciplinary judgement.

Welfare view shows the authorized case; commander view shows the aggregate unit trend only.

The authority can later end/extend the deployment and the operational context changes automatically.
